Hello, I'm Xiaoying Liao.

I am |

I am a Research Assistant in the Qiu Lab at Stanford University, working at the intersection of AI and biology. My research focuses on single-cell and spatial genomics, computational modeling of development and disease, and AI-driven biological discovery.

I have a background in both experimental and computational research. I earned my Master of Biomedical Engineering from Johns Hopkins University and my Bachelor of Science (Honours) in Statistics from the University of Sydney.

I am actively seeking Ph.D. opportunities for Fall 2027.
I am particularly interested in integrating experimental and computational approaches and using AI to discover new biological mechanisms and insights.
